# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 🔎 Executive Summary
This project presents a comprehensive **HR Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Power BI** to analyze workforce composition, employee attrition, age distribution, education background, and job satisfaction trends.  

The dashboard enables HR teams to monitor key workforce metrics and make data-driven strategic decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
The primary objective of this project is to answer critical HR questions:

- What is the total workforce size?
- What is the current attrition rate?
- Which departments experience higher attrition?
- How does attrition vary across age groups and education fields?
- What are the job satisfaction patterns across job roles?

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI**
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
- **Excel (Data Source)**
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation**

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Feature Engineering
To enhance analytical accuracy, the following transformations were performed:

- Converted **Attrition (Yes/No)** into **binary format (1 = Yes, 0 = No)**
- Created structured **Age Group categories**:
  - Under 25  
  - 25–34  
  - 35–44  
  - 45–54  
  - Over 55  
- Added calculated columns for segmentation and KPI calculations
- Standardized categorical fields
- Built DAX measures for dynamic dashboard metrics

---

## 📌 Key HR Metrics
- **Total Employees:** 1470  
- **Active Employees:** 1233  
- **Employees Left:** 237  
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%  
- **Average Employee Age:** 36.92  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights
- **Department-wise Analysis:**  
  R&D shows the highest employee count and attrition, followed by Sales and HR.

- **Age Group Analysis:**  
  The 25–34 age group contributes the highest attrition numbers.

- **Education Field Analysis:**  
  Life Sciences and Medical fields show relatively higher attrition levels.

- **Job Satisfaction Analysis:**  
  Satisfaction levels vary significantly across job roles.
